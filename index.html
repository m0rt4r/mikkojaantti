Who are we?

Speaking are Mikko and Antti. We bring together two different yet complementary perspectives on society, the economy, and pressing current issues.

As the world and the headlines grow increasingly complex, we want to break things down directly and honestly—without unnecessary jargon. Whether the topic is the future of Finnish industry, taxation, or political decisions, we delve deeper than the surface.
